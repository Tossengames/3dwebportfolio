The Craft

Made by hand.
Worn for decades.

Since 1962, every pair of Maison Soulier shoes has been made by a single artisan — from the last to the welt to the final polish. No pair is identical. No two feet are the same.

We use only Grade 1 Italian nappa from the Tuscan tanneries our founder visited in the 1960s. The leather is selected by hand. Imperfections are not cut away — they are worked around.

"A shoe that fits perfectly is one that was made for exactly the body it holds."
— Isabelle Soulier, Creative Director
