Brew Guide

01
Grind Fresh

Grind only what you'll use. Medium-fine for pour-over, coarse for French press.

02
Water Temperature

93–96°C for lighter roasts. 88–91°C for medium profiles. Never boiling.

03
Bloom

Pour 2× the coffee's weight in water. Wait 30–45 seconds. Watch the gas release.

04
Pour & Rest

Slow even circles. Total brew time 3–4 minutes. Rest 60 seconds before tasting.
