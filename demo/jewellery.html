The Atelier

Four generations
of mastery

Since 1887, our artisans in the 1st arrondissement of Paris have worked with the same patience the craft demands — never rushed, never compromised.

Every stone is hand-selected for its cut, clarity, and character. Every setting is made by hand, tested under magnification, and signed by the craftsman who made it.

"A jewel that lasts a thousand years is made in a thousand quiet hours."
— Henri Lumière, Founder
