Rituals of the Season

春

Cherry Blossom Renewal

A 90-minute ceremony of exfoliation, sakura petal bath, and white tea infusion. Designed for the first bloom of the body after winter.

夏

Cool River Meditation

Cold compress ritual, bamboo sound bath, and matcha cold stone massage. The forest in high heat — brought to your skin.

Warm ginger compress, hinoki wood soak, and persimmon oil massage. The closing of the year, drawn across your shoulders.

Wabi-Sabi

The beauty of
imperfect things

We believe that true wellness is not the absence of imperfection, but the acceptance of it. Our rituals are designed not to fix — but to honour.

Every treatment draws from the Japanese concept of Ma — the meaningful pause between moments, where healing truly begins.

"The cherry blossom is beautiful precisely because it falls."

Our practitioners have studied under masters in Kyoto, Osaka, and the ancient mountain onsen of Hakone.
