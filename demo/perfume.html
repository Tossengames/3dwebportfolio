The Maison

A century of
beautiful darkness

Founded in a small atelier on Rue du Faubourg Saint-Honoré in 1924, Obscura has always believed the most compelling fragrances live at the edge of comfort.

"We do not create scents for the day. We create memories for the night that refuses to end."

Every ingredient sourced with obsessive care. Every formula hand-blended in small batches. Every bottle numbered.

— Élise Moreau, Maître Parfumeur
