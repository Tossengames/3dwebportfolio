📖 About Backyard Chickens

A realistic sim to prepare you for real backyard chicken keeping — before you buy your first hen.

🎯 What You'll Learn
  • Daily care routines hens really need
  • Real costs — feed, bedding, vet care, equipment
  • Common health problems and early detection
  • Predator prevention and coop security
  • Seasonal differences in care and egg production
  • Pecking order dynamics and flock behavior
  • Running a small egg business with real customers
⚠️ Consequences of Neglect
🚨 In real life, chickens without water for 24h in summer can die. Hens neglected at night become prey. This game enforces real consequences — check in daily!
⏰ Time

1 real minute ≈ 1 in-game hour. A full day takes 24 real minutes. Seasons change every 7 real-time days of gameplay.

🎓 How to Play

🌍 Overview Mode

Your default view — rotate by dragging, pinch to zoom. Tap any object to interact. See your whole flock at once.

👁️ First Person Mode

Drop into your yard. Left joystick to walk. Right side swipe to look around. Tap nearby objects to interact. WASD + mouse on desktop.

📋 Daily Tasks

Check Tasks every session. Hens need water, food, and the coop locked at dusk. Collect eggs daily or laying slows.

💰 Customers

Build a customer base. Keep them happy with fresh, quality eggs. Neglect = bad reviews = less income!

🦊 Predator Attacks

If a predator comes, switch to First Person and chase it away! Then lock the coop immediately.

🏗️ Yard Upgrades

As your flock grows, upgrade the yard for more space and better equipment.

🌱 Your Starting Flock

3 hens, a basic coop, $200 budget. Name your chickens — keep names short!

💡 Real cost: Starter setup (3 hens + coop) = $150–300. Monthly feed for 3 hens ≈ $15–20.
