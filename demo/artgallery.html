Our Story

Driven by Compulsion

Art is not what you see, but what you make others see.

Art Compulsions is a boutique gallery dedicated to original works that move you — pieces created from pure creative compulsion, not commission. Every painting in our collection carries the unfiltered vision of its maker.

Our 3D viewer lets you experience each work as it would hang in your space — with full control over framing, materials, and lighting so you can find the presentation that speaks to you.
